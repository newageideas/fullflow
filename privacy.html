Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

What we collect

When you use our request form we collect your name, phone number, city, the service you need, your preferred contact time, any notes you write, your language, how you found us (for example a QR code), and — if you check the box — a record of your consent to receive texts, including the time, wording, IP address, and browser type.

How we use it

Only to contact you about your request, schedule service, and send appointment or "on my way" messages if you agreed. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share your mobile number or text consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing.

Text messages

Texting is optional and not required to get service. Message frequency varies by request. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Storage

Requests are stored in a private storage system on our website host (Vercel) and are only accessible to Full Flow Mains and Drains. We keep requests only as long as needed for service and records.
